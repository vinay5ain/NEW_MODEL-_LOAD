    About this report

    Forecast data comes from Open-Meteo. Coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo and this application's Flask backend for nearby CWC station matching.

    This is an indicative rainfall assessment, not a validated urban-flood prediction. Rainfall forecasts alone cannot confirm whether a street will flood. Configured thresholds are illustrative, not official warning thresholds; flooding also depends on verified events, terrain, drainage, and other local conditions.
